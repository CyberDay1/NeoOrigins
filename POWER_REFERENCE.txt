  NeoOrigins - Power Reference for Modpack Creators
================================================================================

  Generated from what this build loads, not written by hand:
    origins  the shipped origin JSON, decoded by Origin.CODEC
    powers   the shipped power JSON, run through PowerDataManager
    types    the power types PowerTypes registers, with the fields
             each type's parser reads
    text     names and descriptions from assets/neoorigins/lang/en_us.json

  Regenerate with:
    ./gradlew test --tests '*PowerReferenceTest' -PwritePowerReference
  PowerReferenceTest fails the build when this file is stale.

  Contents:
    1. Origins and their powers, by layer
    2. Power types
    3. Quick stats

  MERLING
  ID: neoorigins:merling | Impact: MEDIUM | Order: 2 | Icon: minecraft:cod
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  An aquatic people born in the open ocean — breathes water and swims with
  ease, but slow and dries out on dry land.

  neoorigins:merling_coldsweat_cold_resist
    Type: neoorigins:attribute_modifier
    Loads only with mods: cold_sweat

  Gills (neoorigins:merling_water_breathing)
    Type: neoorigins:water_breathing
    Breathes underwater indefinitely and sees clearly through the water with
    no fog.

  Aquatic Speed (neoorigins:merling_aquatic_speed)
    Type: neoorigins:attribute_modifier
    Moves faster while swimming.

  Open Water (neoorigins:aquatic_swim_speed)
    Type: neoorigins:attribute_modifier
    Swims strongly clear of the seafloor, not just along it.

  Neutral Buoyancy (neoorigins:aquatic_buoyancy)
    Type: neoorigins:attribute_modifier
    Holds depth when fully submerged instead of sinking. Swimming down still
    works normally.

  Pescivore (neoorigins:aquatic_fish_diet)
    Type: neoorigins:action_on_event
    Can only eat fish. Server admins can disable this in config
    (ocean_origins.fish_diet_required).

  Raw Adapted (neoorigins:aquatic_fish_diet_bonus)
    Type: neoorigins:action_on_event
    Raw cod and salmon are as nourishing as the cooked versions.

  Deep Sight (neoorigins:merling_night_vision)
    Type: neoorigins:persistent_effect (written as neoorigins:night_vision)
    Sees clearly in the dark depths. Toggle with the Night Vision key (default
    K).

  Landlubber (neoorigins:merling_land_slowdown)
    Type: neoorigins:attribute_modifier
    Moves 10% slower on land.

  Dries Out (neoorigins:merling_dries_out)
    Type: neoorigins:breath_out_of_fluid
    Air ticks down while you're out of water: surface too long and you
    suffocate. Rain, a water cauldron, Water Breathing or a nearby active
    conduit all hold it off.

  Aqua Affinity (neoorigins:aquatic_underwater_mining)
    Type: neoorigins:underwater_mining_speed
    Mines at full speed while submerged.

  Natural Swimmer (neoorigins:aquatic_depth_strider)
    Type: neoorigins:attribute_modifier
    Moves slightly faster through water.

  Tier 1 adds:

  Evolved Vitality (neoorigins:merling_evolved_hp)
    Type: neoorigins:attribute_modifier
    +2 max health.

  Streamlined (neoorigins:merling_evolved_swim_speed)
