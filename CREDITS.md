## Origin design inspiration

NeoOrigins is a ground-up reimplementation and contains no code from any other
Origins addon. Much of what it ships beyond the vanilla Origins set arrived as
community suggestions, and some of those suggestions may have been shaped by
addons that covered the same ground first. Where an origin here lines up with
one of theirs, the idea is theirs:

- **Extra Origins** by MoriyaShiine —
  [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/extra-origins)
- **Medieval Origins Revival** by muon-rw / MuonR, itself a port of Medieval
  Origins by ItsParkieLad —
  [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/medieval-origins-revival) ·
  [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/medieval-origins-revival)
- **Mob Origins** by UltrusBot —
  [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/moborigins) ·
  [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mob-origins)
- **Mobs Origins** —
  [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/mobs-origins)
- **Origins: Vampire** by Venekiel —
  [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/datapack/origins-vampire) ·
  [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/origins-vampire)

If you maintain one of these and would like the wording changed, or your project
removed from this list, open an issue and it will be handled.
