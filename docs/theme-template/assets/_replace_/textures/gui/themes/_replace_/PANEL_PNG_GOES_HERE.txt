Drop a file named 'panel.png' here (delete this .txt placeholder once you do).

Recommended specs:
  - 256x256 PNG (any RGBA palette)
  - The outer 12 px ring on every side acts as the 9-slice border. Put the
    organic / textured part in that ring; the inner area is stretched to fill
    the panel and should look fine smeared.
  - If your texture isn't 256x256 or wants a different border thickness, edit
    texture_width / texture_height / inset_* in ui_themes/my_theme.json.

If you don't ship your own panel.png, the theme still works — it just keeps
the built-in parchment panel (because panel_background defaults to
neoorigins:textures/gui/themes/parchment/panel.png if you remove the field).
